# Land patterns

KiCad ships no footprint matching any of the three parts below, so all three
are generated by `tools/gen_footprints.py` from the dimensions recorded here.
Every value comes from the manufacturer's recommended land pattern.

KiCad does carry six full-size SD sockets in `Connector_Card`, from TE, Hirose
and Kyocera, but none share the SD-006M's contact count, shell tabs or peg
positions. Full-size SD sockets are not interchangeable between manufacturers.

Coordinates are millimetres in each footprint's own frame, Y positive downward.

## SD-006M

SOFNG full-size SD socket, push-push, LCSC C125615. Datasheet page 2,
"CIRCUIT BOARD SIZE (Vertical view)".

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Contact row → peg holes | 24.60 |
| Peg hole spacing | 24.20, at ±12.10 |
| Peg hole diameter | 1.60 non-plated (specified 1.50) |
| GROUND1 → peg holes, Y | 2.10 |
| GROUND2 above GROUND1 | 1.20 |
| Contact pad | 1.1989 × 2.1996 (specified 1.20 × 2.00) |
| Shell tab pad | 1.999 × 2.3012 (specified 1.70 × 2.00) |

Contact X positions, referenced to pad 9:

| Contact | Offset from #9 |
|---|---|
| #1 | 2.50 |
| #2 | 5.00 |
| CD | 6.43 |
| #3 | 8.30 |
| #4 | 10.00 |
| #5 | 12.50 |
| #6 | 15.00 |
| #7 | 17.43 |
| #8 | 19.13 |
| WP | 22.38 |

The row is not evenly pitched. Gaps run 2.50 between #6, #5 and #4, then 2.43
from #7 to #6, 1.70 from #8 to #7, and 3.25 from WP to #8.

The two shell tabs are not mirror images. The right tab sits further out and
1.20 mm nearer the contact row than the left.

Pads are drawn larger than specified, contacts by 0.20 in length and shell tabs
by 0.30 in both axes. The extra area is solder fillet and is deliberate.

The socket body is 29.0 × 28.5 × 2.9 mm. Its two front corners overhang the
board edge by up to 1.14 mm over the last 1.3 mm of its length, where the board
outline has not yet widened past the 28.5 mm body.

## USB-AM90

Shou Han right-angle USB-A male plug, LCSC C404965. Drawing A/0 dated
2018.07.10, PCB layout view.

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Signal pin spacing | 2.50 / 2.00 / 2.50, spanning 7.00 |
| Signal hole diameter | 1.00 |
| Pin row → peg and tab row | 2.00 |
| Locating peg spacing | 4.50, at ±2.25 |
| Locating peg diameter | 1.00 non-plated |
| Shell tab spacing | 11.70, at ±5.855 |
| Shell tab opening | 0.90 × 2.25 plated slot (specified 0.86 × 2.20) |

The AM90 has no mounting posts. Its shell ends in two flat 0.86 mm tabs that
belong in slots, and those tabs are the only mechanical anchorage for a board
that cantilevers roughly 75 mm out of a USB port. Slots are cut 0.04 mm over the
specified opening to leave room for hole plating.

Peg clearance is tight. A 0.85 mm peg in a 1.00 mm hole leaves 0.05-0.075 mm
radially, so the 2.00 mm offset from the pin row must be held or the plug will
bind rather than seat flat.

## LED-12-215SYGC

Everlight 12-215SYGC/S530-E2/TR8 yellow-green chip LED, LCSC C131283.

| Dimension | Value |
|---|---|
| Body | 2.0 × 1.0 × 0.8, an 0804 |
| Pad | 0.8992 × 0.8001 at ±1.0503 |
| Polarity | pad 1 is the cathode, on -x |

An 0804 is outside the metric chip series, so no stock land fits. The terminals
sit 0.60-1.00 mm out from the centre, which a 1.6 mm 0603 land does not reach
properly.

The footprint carries a silkscreen bar outside pad 1. Chip LEDs have no legible
marking once placed, so the board has to state polarity.

## U1

`U1` uses stock `Package_SO:SSOP-16_3.9x4.9mm_P0.635mm`: pads 1.65 × 0.40 on
0.635 mm pitch with rows at ±2.625. IPC-correct for the package.

Pad rotation angles are stored absolutely in KiCad, with the footprint's
placement rotation baked in. `U1` sits at 270 degrees, so its instance carries
pad angles its library copy does not.

Running `Tools → Update Footprints from Library` strips those angles and shorts
the board: the pad positions stay rotated while the pad shapes do not, and
`U1`'s 1.65 mm pads then lie across a 0.635 mm pitch. Re-run
`tools/gen_pcb.py` instead, and re-check DRC.

## Regenerating

`gen_footprints.py` derives each courtyard from the pad extents, so any pad
change also changes the courtyard. Board footprint instances carry their own
copy of that rectangle. After re-running the generator, sync pad positions and
both `fp_rect` entries in each instance, then refill zones with
`kicad-cli pcb drc --refill-zones --save-board`. Stale zone fill otherwise
appears as clearance errors around whatever moved.
